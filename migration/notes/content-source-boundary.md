# Content source boundary

The public KULT360 website will become the primary source of truth only after explicit extraction authorization. Phase 1 performs no crawl, download, copying, import, or source-site inspection.

During Phase 2, record provenance before transformation, prefer the highest-resolution public source rather than generated thumbnails, preserve page-to-asset relationships, distinguish KULT360 photography from partner/institutional marks, deduplicate without losing provenance, preserve old URLs for redirects, and flag uncertain/obsolete/placeholder material instead of publishing it automatically. Missing facts must remain missing.
